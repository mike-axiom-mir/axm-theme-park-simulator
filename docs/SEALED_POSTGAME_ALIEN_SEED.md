# Sealed Postgame Alien Continuation

Status: preserved future seed. Do not distort the realistic Earth foundation to
implement it early.

After the final Earth campaign, the player may end the game or remain in the park.
A strange visitor then tests the completed park. Following it with the camera
produces a hidden assessment and alien-resonance result. At a sufficient threshold,
the player receives unfamiliar currency and an imperfect translated invitation.

Accepting begins a small alien park with unfamiliar senses, movement, social
behavior, comfort, risk, time, value exchange, and attraction purposes. The player
must observe behavior, form hypotheses, change one variable, test again, and grow
confidence.

Core inversion:

> Earth teaches the player to build for people they understand. Space tests
> whether they can build responsibly for beings they do not.

Unknown does not mean irrational; it means the causes have not yet been understood.
The branch must reuse deterministic events, evidence, uncertainty, state-bound
inspection, and multiple campaign endings.
