# Uncertainty and Evidence

Evidence is not decoration around a conclusion. It is part of the result.

A claim carries:

- statement;
- confidence from 0 to 1;
- evidence references;
- alternative explanations;
- missing evidence or uncertainty notes;
- conflict relationships where relevant.

High confidence must not be manufactured by hiding alternatives. A beginner card
may shorten the wording but must preserve the claim confidence and evidence link.

This becomes especially important in later unknown-species campaigns: repeated
behavior can support a hypothesis without instantly revealing a hidden preference
as fact.
