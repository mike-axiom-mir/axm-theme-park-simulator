# Sealed Postgame Science-Fiction Seed

Status: preserved future branch; not part of first-map scope.

After the final grounded Earth campaign, the player may end normally or remain
in the park. A strange visitor enters the authoritative guest system and tests
the completed park. Following it with the camera creates a hidden assessment and
alien-resonance result.

At a threshold, the visitor sends an imperfect translated message, transfers
unfamiliar currency, and asks the player to follow.

Accepting begins a genuinely alien park:

- unfamiliar senses;
- unfamiliar movement and social behavior;
- incomplete translation;
- non-human comfort, risk, value, and repetition;
- behavior learned through observation and controlled experiments.

Core mechanic:

build or change something
→ observe approach and reaction
→ compare groups
→ form a hypothesis
→ change one variable
→ test again
→ increase confidence

Root:

> Unknown does not mean irrational. It means the causes have not yet been
> understood.

The expansion must not be an Earth park with alien skins. It uses the same causal
and uncertainty foundation while hiding meanings the player must discover.
