# Audience Cohorts and Market Conservation

## Why cohorts exist

The park must not spawn generic popularity units. A reachable geographic audience
contains real people in different relationships with the park.

The reference lifecycle separates:

- unaware;
- aware;
- considering;
- first-time ready;
- previously satisfied;
- loyal repeat;
- disappointed.

Every step conserves the cohort population.

## Causal transitions

Awareness, consideration, travel/price readiness, first-visit rate, repeat rate,
repeat strength, expectation delivery, loyalty, recovery, and visit capacity are
separate forces.

Marketing moves unaware people toward awareness. It does not manufacture people.
An old attraction can remain new to the first-time reservoir. Repeat demand comes
from prior satisfaction and loyalty rather than a global ride-age score.

Recovery repairs willingness and trust but does not rewrite history. A person who
has already visited remains in the repeat reservoir; they can never flow back
through awareness and be presented as a first-time visitor again.

## Capacity

First-time and repeat demand compete for real visit capacity. Unserved demand is
reported, not silently discarded. Later production modules can make allocation
policy park-specific while preserving the same conservation invariant.

## Scale

The reference cohort is aggregate. `PopulationResolutionPolicy` can represent
small populations individually and larger populations in exact buckets without
changing the total represented people.
