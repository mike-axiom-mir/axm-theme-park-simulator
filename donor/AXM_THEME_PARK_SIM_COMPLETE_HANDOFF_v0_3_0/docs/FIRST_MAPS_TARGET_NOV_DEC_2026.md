# First Maps Target — November–December 2026

Status: target window, not guaranteed promise.

## Purpose

Reach a small playable park early enough to test:

- whether the tycoon loop is understandable;
- whether deterministic causes feel fair;
- whether entering the park adds atmosphere;
- whether AXM can build and improve its own low-graphic animations;
- whether the branch contracts cooperate in a real map.

## Minimum meaningful slice

- one compact map;
- at least one entrance and path network;
- a small attraction set using simple movement;
- visitor arrival, walking, queueing, riding, and leaving;
- opening/closing time;
- one weather or day-context change;
- one crew or maintenance consequence;
- basic cost/revenue;
- save and deterministic replay;
- beginner explanation card;
- first-person or close inspection mode;
- clear in-production and concept-art labelling.

## Quality rule

A fun readable loop is more important than visual polish.

Low graphics are acceptable when:

- they are self-made or source-provenanced;
- movement communicates state;
- text does not pretend the build is final;
- visuals improve through measured passes;
- placeholder assets can be replaced without breaking simulation identity.

## Scope guard

Do not delay first maps for:

- complete physics;
- every ride type;
- advanced resorts;
- giant campaign;
- final art;
- every visitor trait;
- alien content.
