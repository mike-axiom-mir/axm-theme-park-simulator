# Decision Log — 2026-08-04

Status: WORKING CANON for foundation v0.1.0

1. The project begins with the shared deterministic foundation before domain
   modules are built.
2. Age-based global popularity decay is rejected.
3. Novelty is audience/visitor-specific.
4. Attractions are evaluated through direct and whole-park contribution.
5. Geography and reachable new audiences are foundational.
6. Simultaneous active attendance and park scale affect sustainability.
7. Theming is optional but can substantially change total experience.
8. Theme influence must be coherent and evidence-based, not decoration spam.
9. Marketing targets audiences and creates expectations.
10. Campaigns are inherited living systems with multiple valid futures.
11. Adventure and management use one shared simulation state.
12. Beginner guidance sits above, not instead of, the deep simulation.
13. Four primary build branches are defined: rides; visitors/crews;
    decoration/influence; stores/services/economy.
