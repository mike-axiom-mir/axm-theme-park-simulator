AXM Theme Park Simulator — IN PRODUCTION

A long-term theme-park simulation and AXM low-graphic animation testing ground.

The first versions aim for reachable, self-made low-graphic movement and
animation. The systems, visuals, and park experiences can improve step by step.

Core direction:
- no fake popularity decay;
- maintain, evolve, or replace with intention;
- visitors react through causes;
- build from above and experience the park from within;
- parks can grow history instead of forced demolition cycles.

First maps are currently targeted for November/December 2026. This is a target,
not a guaranteed release date.

Concept visuals are not gameplay screenshots.
