Manage

Choose a build item, point at the globe, and click. Drag paths to paint a route. R rotates. Left-drag rotates the camera; right-drag/WASD pans; wheel zooms.

WASD or left stick moves, Q/E, mouse, or right stick turns, Shift/trigger runs, and Space/A inspects or collects glowing trail stamps. Click guests or visible crew to read their live needs and current work.
