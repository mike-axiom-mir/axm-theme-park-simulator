AXM THEME PARK — PLAYABLE LIVING GLOBE 3D v0.4.6
=================================================

STATUS
------

walkable Living Globe, visible routed crew, spatial litter, a 3D opening camera
flight, state-aware ride/boarding motion, distinct animated service buildings,
visible service customers, decorated paths and queues, four animated scenery

The ready build needs neither internet nor installation.

FIRST TEST ROUTE
----------------
1. Choose Start campaign park and let the opening settle into the live globe.
2. Inspect normal paths and queue lanes for inset stones, verge plants, swaying
   flowers, and fluttering pennants.
3. Place a tree, lantern, fountain, and bench; watch each unique animation.
4. Fast-forward until Guest Pulse reports Rest, follow a signaled guest to a
   bench, and confirm queue, seated recovery, and return to the park.
5. Compare guest-signal density at Crisp, Retro, and Tiny.
6. Watch butterflies before 18:00 and fireflies after 18:00; trigger rain and
   confirm both yield while windblown leaves remain tree-anchored.
7. Watch one ride board/cycle/close sequence and one customer at each service.
8. Finish a day, inspect Bench rests, then save/reload and confirm continuity.
